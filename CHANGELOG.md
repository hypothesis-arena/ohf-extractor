### 0.9.14-dev

- The ohf-extractor kit turns one published biology article into a source-linked Open Hypothesis Format card.
- It keeps the authors' quotations separate from the converter's additions, records gaps with reasons, and prepares inputs for a separate entailment check.
- Its scripts check the card structure, quotations against retained source bytes, references, assessment times and recorded check results.
